Tips to get it right

  • Check the number at the bottom of the card: the same card can exist in different versions (regular, reverse holo, full art) at very different prices.
  • On Cardmarket look at the "price trend" and filter by the same language and condition as your card.
  • A graded card (PSA, BGS, CGC) is worth much more than the same "raw" card: only compare with the same grade.

Why do prices change so much?

Card values depend on rarity, condition, language and current demand: a new set release or collectors' interest in a particular Pokémon can move prices within days. The most reliable reference is recent real sales of the same card. The calculator automatically removes outliers (less than half or more than double the median).

How is condition graded?

Near Mint means practically perfect, as if fresh from the pack. Excellent and Good mean small marks on the edges or back. A card with creases, obvious scratches or damaged corners is "played" and worth much less.

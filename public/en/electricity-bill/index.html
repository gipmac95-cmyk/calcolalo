How is the electricity cost calculated?

The yearly cost is your usage in kWh multiplied by the average price you pay per kWh, including fixed charges, network costs, system charges, excise duty and VAT. Using an "all-in" price is the simplest way to get a reliable estimate: the real bill varies a little depending on when you use energy and on your tariff (fixed or variable price).

How much electricity does a household use?

A household of 3 uses on average about 2,000–2,500 kWh a year. Homes with a heat pump, induction hob or electric car use much more. You can find the exact figure on your bill under "consumo annuo".

Where can I compare offers?

The Portale Offerte run by ARERA, the public energy regulator, compares all free-market offers neutrally. Private comparison sites are handy, but only show the suppliers they have agreements with.

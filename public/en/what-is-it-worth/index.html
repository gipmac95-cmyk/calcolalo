Tips for selling at the right price

  • On eBay look at completed sales: those are the prices actually paid. On Vinted and Subito you see asking prices, usually a bit higher.
  • Only compare items identical to yours: same model, same size or capacity, same condition.
  • Clear photos, an honest description and the original box push the price up.

How does it work?

Type the name of the item: we take you straight to results on the main second-hand sites and to new prices. If you enter the new price, we give a rough estimate based on age, condition and type of item: electronics lose value fast, while designer clothing and collectables hold it better. Finally you can paste the prices you find to get the market average, without outliers.

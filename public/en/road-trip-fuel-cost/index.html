How do you work out the cost of a car journey?

Fuel cost is the distance multiplied by the car's consumption and the price per litre. With a consumption of 6.5 litres per 100 km and petrol at €1.95 per litre (the Italian average in November 2026), 300 km cost about €38 in fuel. Add motorway tolls (most Italian motorways are tolled) and parking.

How do I find my car's real fuel consumption?

The most reliable figure is your own: fill the tank, reset the trip meter and at the next fill-up divide the litres you put in by the kilometres driven, then multiply by 100. The manufacturer's official figure is usually lower than real-world consumption.

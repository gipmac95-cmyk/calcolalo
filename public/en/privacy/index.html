Privacy and cookies

How VuoiSapereQuantoCosta.it handles visitors' data. Last updated: November 2026.

Data controller

Marco, who runs VuoiSapereQuantoCosta.it. For any privacy request you can write to gipmac95@gmail.com (gipmac95@gmail.com).

What you type into the calculators

All calculations happen directly in your browser. The numbers and words you type into the calculators and the search box are not sent to us or stored by us.

Technical cookies

We only store your cookie choice in your browser, so we don't have to ask you again on every visit. No consent is needed for this.

Statistics (Google Analytics)

Only if you click "Accept", we use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to count visits, page views, device type, traffic sources and clicks to other websites in aggregate form. This helps us understand which pages are useful and improve them. Google may also process data in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded. You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which may record data such as IP addresses in server logs for technical and security reasons.

Links to other websites

Many pages link to external websites (for example eBay, Vinted, Subito, ARERA, energy and internet providers). When you open them, those websites' privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to processing and lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

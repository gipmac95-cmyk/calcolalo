The estimate is based on a statistical depreciation model. The real price depends on trim, options, colour and area: always check listings for the same model.

How to read the listings

  • Filter by the same year (±1), similar mileage and the same engine.
  • Listing prices are asking prices, not final prices: deals usually close 5–10% lower.
  • Ignore listings far above or below the rest: they are often mistakes or cars with problems.

How do we estimate your car's value?

On average a car loses about 15–20% of its value in the first year and then a smaller share each year: after 5 years it is typically worth around 60% of the price when new, after 10 years just over a third. On top of age-related depreciation we adjust for mileage (against an average of 15,000 km a year), fuel type and condition.

Private sale, trade-in or dealer?

A private sale gets you the most. A trade-in pays less, because the dealer has to prepare the car, give a warranty and make a profit; in exchange it's hassle-free. The price at which a dealer resells the same car is higher, because it includes warranty and preparation.

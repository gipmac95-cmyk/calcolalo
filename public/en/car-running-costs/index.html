How much does it cost to keep a car in Italy?

Between fuel, insurance, car tax (bollo), servicing, tyres and the MOT (revisione), a car usually costs a few thousand euros a year in Italy. On top of that comes depreciation: the loss in value is often the biggest cost, even though you don't see it until you sell.

How is depreciation calculated?

The calculator assumes a used car loses about 10% of its value each year, plus a share linked to mileage. New cars lose more in the first years. For a more precise estimate of your car's value use the "What is my car worth?" calculator.

How can I cut the cost of my car?

The biggest savings are on insurance, by comparing quotes every year, and on fuel, by choosing the cheapest stations and driving smoothly. Regular servicing avoids expensive breakdowns and keeps resale value higher.

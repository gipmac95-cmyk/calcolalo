How much is the assegno unico in 2026?

For each child under 18 the base amount ranges from €203.80 a month with an ISEE up to €17,468.51, to €58.30 with an ISEE of €46,582.71 or more, or without an ISEE. For children aged 18 to 20 it ranges from €99.10 to €29.10. Between the two thresholds the amount decreases gradually.

What supplements are there?

For children under one the amount increases by 50%; the same applies to children aged 1 to 3 in families with at least three children and an ISEE up to €46,582.71. There are also supplements from the third child on, for each child when both parents work, for mothers under 21, and a flat €150 a month for households with at least four children.

Do I need an ISEE?

No, the benefit is paid even without an ISEE (the Italian household means-test indicator), but in that case you get the minimum amount. If you submit an updated ISEE by 30 June you also recover arrears back to March.

How do we estimate gas usage?

We count about 70 cubic metres a year for cooking and about 130 cubic metres per person for hot water. Heating is the biggest item: it depends on floor area, your local climate and how well the home is insulated. An 80 m² home in the Po Valley with average insulation uses about 900 cubic metres just for heating.

How can I save on heating?

Each degree lower on the thermostat saves about 6–7% of the gas used for heating. Thermostatic valves, boiler servicing and well-sealed windows help a lot. Switching supplier can also save money: ARERA's Portale Offerte compares all free-market offers neutrally.

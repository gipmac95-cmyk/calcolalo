Is an electric car really cheaper?

An electric car usually costs more to buy, but less to fuel and maintain, since servicing is simpler: no engine oil, spark plugs, timing belt or clutch. The more kilometres you drive and the more often you charge at home, the better the deal. The calculator shows after how many years the yearly savings pay back the higher purchase price.

How much does it cost to charge an electric car in Italy?

It depends a lot on where you charge. At home you pay your electricity bill rate, lower with dedicated tariffs or solar panels. At public chargers the pay-as-you-go price is higher and rises with charging speed; apps and subscriptions bring it down. At 16 kWh per 100 km, every extra 10 cents per kWh costs €1.60 more per 100 km.

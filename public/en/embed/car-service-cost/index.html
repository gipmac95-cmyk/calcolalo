How much does a car service cost in Italy in 2026?

For a car, a basic service (oil, oil filter and checks) usually costs between €150 and €400, depending on the model and the garage. A full service with all filters costs more. For a mid-size motorbike a basic service is usually €150–200, a 125 cc scooter less. Electric cars cost less to maintain because they have no engine oil, spark plugs or timing belt.

How is the estimate calculated?

Every job has two parts: parts and labour. Labour is the expected hours of work multiplied by the garage's hourly rate. Independent garages in Italy charge on average about €45–60 an hour plus VAT (IVA, 22%); main dealers charge more. The calculator adds parts and labour, VAT included, and shows a range from the cheapest to the most expensive case.

When should the timing belt be replaced?

Each manufacturer sets an interval in kilometres and years, often between 60,000 and 160,000 km. It's one of the most expensive jobs, but putting it off is risky: if the belt snaps the engine can be badly damaged, with far more expensive repairs. The tensioner and water pump are usually replaced at the same time.

How much does the Italian MOT (revisione) cost?

The revisione (roadworthiness test) has a fixed price set by law, the same throughout Italy: at authorised private test centres it costs €79.02, for both cars and motorbikes. It is due 4 years after first registration and then every 2 years.

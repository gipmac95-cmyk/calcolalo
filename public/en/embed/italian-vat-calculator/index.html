How do you remove VAT from a price?

To get the net amount, divide the VAT-inclusive price by (1 + rate/100). With 22% VAT: €122 ÷ 1.22 = €100 net and €22 VAT. Careful: simply taking 22% off the gross price gives the wrong result (€122 − 22% = €95.16).

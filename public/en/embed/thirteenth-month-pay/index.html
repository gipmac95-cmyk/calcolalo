How is the 13th-month pay calculated?

The gross tredicesima is one twelfth of your gross monthly salary for each month worked: with 12 months it equals one full gross monthly salary. The employee's INPS contributions are deducted, then IRPEF on the rest according to the 2026 brackets: 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,000 to €50,000 and 43% above.

Why is the net 13th-month pay lower than a normal salary?

Employee and dependant tax credits are not applied to the tredicesima, because they are already spread over the normal monthly payments. So IRPEF is paid in full at your bracket's rate and the net amount is lower than a normal month. Regional and municipal surtaxes are not withheld on the tredicesima.

When is the 13th-month pay paid?

Usually with the December payslip, generally by 24 December. Exact timing depends on the collective agreement and the employer. In 2026 there is no special tax cut on the tredicesima: ordinary rules apply.

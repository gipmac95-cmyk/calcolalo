How does the comparison work?

From the gas you use today we work out how much heat your home really needs: each cubic metre of gas contains about 10.7 kWh of energy, part of which the boiler turns into heat depending on its efficiency. The heat pump produces the same heat using electricity: with a SCOP of 3.2 it delivers 3.2 kWh of heat for every kWh of electricity.

When is a heat pump worth it?

It pays off most in well-insulated homes with underfloor heating or large radiators working at low temperature, and when electricity is cheap compared with gas, for example with a dedicated tariff or solar panels. With small radiators and a poorly insulated home efficiency drops and so does the saving.

Why isn't the advertised price enough?

Many Italian internet offers are discounted only for the first months, then the price goes up. On top of that come the activation fee and the router, often paid in instalments. To really compare two offers you need to add everything up over the same period: the calculator does it for you and shows the real average monthly cost.

Should I check coverage before choosing?

Yes. Speed depends on the technology available at your address (full fibre FTTH, fibre-to-the-cabinet FTTC, or FWA via radio). Check coverage on the provider's website by entering your address. After activation you can measure your real speed with the free tool from AGCOM, the Italian communications regulator.

How is TFR calculated?

Each year the employer sets aside the annual salary divided by 13.5, minus 0.5% of salary that goes to INPS. The TFR already accrued is revalued each year by 1.5% plus 75% of inflation. When paid out, TFR is taxed at a separately calculated average rate (tassazione separata), so the net amount is lower than the gross figure.

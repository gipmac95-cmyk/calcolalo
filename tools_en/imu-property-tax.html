Before you pay

  • Check your municipality's exact rate: many apply different rates for rented properties, regulated-rent contracts or homes lent to a child.
  • You pay with the F24 form (tax code 3918 for other buildings, 3912 for a luxury main home). Amounts are rounded to the nearest euro.
  • If you paid the whole amount in June, in December you only pay any top-up due to a rate increase.

How is IMU calculated?

Start from the cadastral income (rendita catastale), increase it by 5% and multiply it by the category coefficient (160 for homes). The result is the taxable value, to which the municipal rate applies. Example: income €600 × 1.05 × 160 = €100,800 taxable value; at a 1.06% rate yearly IMU is €1,068.48.

When is the 2026 IMU balance due?

The first instalment was due on 16 June 2026 and was calculated with the 2025 rates. The balance must be paid by Wednesday 16 December 2026 and settles the difference with the rates set for 2026: if your municipality hasn't changed them, it is half the yearly tax.

Do you pay IMU on your main home?

No, your main home and its annexes (one each in categories C/2, C/6 and C/7) are exempt. The exception is luxury homes in categories A/1, A/8 and A/9, which pay at a reduced rate with a €200 deduction.

How is a loan payment calculated?

With French amortisation the payment is always the same: payment = amount × i / (1 − (1 + i)−n), where i is the TAN divided by 12 and n the number of months. At first the payment is mostly interest, then the principal share grows.

What is the difference between TAN and TAEG?

TAN is the nominal interest rate. TAEG (the Italian APR) is the real cost of the loan, because it also includes arrangement fees, payment collection fees and other compulsory costs. To compare two offers always look at the TAEG: a low TAN with high fees can cost more than a higher TAN with no fees.

Is dealer car finance a good deal?

It depends on the TAEG and the terms. "Zero interest" offers often have a TAEG above zero because of fees, or a large balloon payment at the end. Always compare the dealer's offer with a personal loan and with the cash price.

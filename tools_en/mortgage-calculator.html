How is the mortgage payment calculated?

With French amortisation, the standard in Italy, the payment stays the same for the whole term. In the early years it is mostly interest, then the principal share grows. The formula is: payment = P × i / (1 − (1 + i)−n), where P is the principal, i the monthly rate and n the number of payments.

What is the difference between TAN and TAEG?

TAN is the nominal interest rate on the loan. TAEG (the Italian APR) also includes the extra costs and is the right figure to compare offers from different banks.

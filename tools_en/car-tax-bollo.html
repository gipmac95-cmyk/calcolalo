How is the Italian car tax calculated?

The bollo is charged per kW of engine power, at a rate that depends on the Euro class. For a Euro 4, 5 or 6 car the standard rate is €2.58 per kW up to 100 kW and €3.87 for each kW above 100. A 51 kW Euro 6 small car therefore pays €131.58 a year. If the kW figure has decimals, only the whole number counts (except for electric, LPG and methane cars).

Does every region have its own rules?

Yes. The bollo is a regional tax: some regions add a surcharge, others offer discounts or exemptions (for example for electric, hybrid or LPG cars). The result here is the national standard rate; you can check the exact amount on the ACI (Automobile Club d'Italia) calculator or your region's website.

What is the superbollo?

Cars with more than 185 kW pay an extra €20 for every kW above that threshold. The surcharge drops to 60% after 5 years from first registration, 30% after 10 years and 15% after 15 years; after 20 years it is no longer due.

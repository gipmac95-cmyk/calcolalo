How is the IPT calculated?

For cars up to 53 kW the IPT (Imposta Provinciale di Trascrizione) is a flat €150.81. Above 53 kW you pay €3.5119 for every kW of the car's total power. Provinces can raise the amount by up to 30%, and most of them do.

Who pays for the ownership transfer?

The law does not say: usually the buyer pays, but buyer and seller can agree otherwise. It's best to write it down in the sale agreement.

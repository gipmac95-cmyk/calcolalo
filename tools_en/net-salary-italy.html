How is net salary calculated in Italy?

From the gross annual salary (RAL) you first deduct the employee's INPS social security contributions (usually 9.19%, plus 1% on the part above €56,224 in 2026). IRPEF income tax is then charged on the rest: 23% up to €28,000, 33% from €28,000 to €50,000 and 43% above. Employee tax credits are deducted from IRPEF, then regional and municipal surtaxes are added.

What is the tax wedge cut?

Employees earning up to €20,000 receive a tax-free bonus equal to a percentage of income (7.1%, 5.3% or 4.8% depending on the band). Between €20,000 and €32,000 there is an extra €1,000 tax credit, which gradually falls to zero at €40,000.
